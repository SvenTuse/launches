# Why CRUMBS Sent

Crumbs made the Robinhood Chain stock-token narrative immediately understandable by promising up to 5% back from ordinary shopping receipts, with familiar Costco, Apple and Netflix examples turning discarded proof of spending into a compelling rewards story.
Its public X campaign concentrated attention in a 1.2M-view prelaunch video and a 517K-view launch video, reinforced by creator explanations, an automated listing and ecosystem coverage, while mint-green phone renders, glossy coins and the recurring pixel-C identity made the concept recognizable; paid promotion was not established.
The announced 75% fee allocation to buybacks and burns and claimed 3% supply burn gave that attention a plausible speculative-demand thesis alongside the reported $7.97M peak capitalization and $20.06M lifetime trading volume, although causal attribution, executed fee-funded buybacks and project profits were not independently verified.
