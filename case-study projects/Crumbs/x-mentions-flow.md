## Marketing channels and what the evidence establishes

1. **Official social distribution:** explainer videos, mobile UI renders, merchant logos, a dog joke, usage updates and burn claims; five official examples occur in search, so their reach must not be counted twice across folders.
2. **Third-party creator posts:** GreenKatanaFX reuses the launch video; Mr Jack uses the same glossy mint disc motif; SpiderCrypto translates the product into a Honey analogy and discloses a position. These are visible publication channels. None shows a paid partnership label or evidence of compensation, so “organic-looking posts; payment unknown” is the defensible classification.
3. **Automated discovery:** Fourtis’s 9.3K-view listing animation surfaces the CA, price and market cap in a robotic-hand creative; no sponsorship is established.
4. **Ecosystem/community media:** RH Daily’s 2.2K-view roundup places the pixel-C logo beside other ecosystem projects, lending recognizability through juxtaposition. Official Robinhood affiliation is unverified.
5. **Unverified voting/giveaway solicitation:** eight near-identical Sep 20–22 messages together have 143 captured views. This establishes repetition, not successful distribution, an actual listing, legitimate incentives, or affiliation. They cannot be assigned to the launch campaign without further evidence.
6. **Critical distribution:** Wazz’s images use dark dashboards, orange wallet diagrams and numerical loss/extraction headlines; other critics use red “SCAM” overlays and repeated-team comparisons. These later narratives could reinforce distrust, but the screenshots alone cannot verify their forensic conclusions.

Paid advertising, sponsored placements, incentivized user-created content and mainstream press coverage are not established. The highest corpus figure outside the launch duplicates includes a 71K-view Bliss warning and a 26K-view DEED warning; treating them as direct CRUMBS promotion would exaggerate the launch funnel. Truncated “Show more” passages and tiny diagram text were never reconstructed as if fully visible.
