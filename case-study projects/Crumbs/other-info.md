# Crumbs — Additional Information and Source Checks

Research date: **September 27, 2026**. Contract match is required throughout: `0x80baa4b3bfac6f4978700df824b1b3d98e889136`. Search covered the name, ticker, contract, account handle and domain; broad name results include unrelated businesses and unrelated tokens.

## 1. External market listing with an exact contract match

[OpenSea’s exact-contract token page](https://opensea.io/token/robinhood/0x80baa4b3bfac6f4978700df824b1b3d98e889136) identifies CRUMBS on Robinhood Chain. The opened page reports **1B total supply**, a recorded **price ATH of $0.005987 on Sep 8, 2026**, approximately **$42.5K FDV**, **4,717 holders**, **$4,233.48 one-day volume**, and **$45.4K seven-day volume**. These are retrieved-page snapshots; the index first returned a different, earlier-looking snapshot ($138.4K FDV, 5,081 holders and $16.7M seven-day volume), so neither is treated as an immutable current quote. On the stated 1B supply basis, the opened-page ATH price implies approximately **$5.987M FDV**, which differs from the supplied **$7.97M capitalization ATH**. Market cap, FDV and cumulative volume must not be treated as revenue or profit.

## 2. Corroborating directories, and disagreement between aggregators

[RootData’s Crumbs page](https://www.rootdata.com/projects/detail/Crumbs?k=MjU3MzQ%3D) describes the receipt-reward project and links its domain/X/Telegram. The indexed result records a Sep 9, 2026 trading-launch event, **1B supply**, a **$0.001995 price ATH**, approximately **$29.64K FDV**, and **13.04K followers / 6 following**. Its team, funding and audit fields do not identify verifiable counterparties. The price ATH implies **$1.995M** on the stated supply basis; it does not reconcile the supplied capitalization peak. Opening the page timed out, so these details are specifically indexed observations rather than a verified live page session.

[CryptoRank’s Crumbs page](https://cryptorank.io/price/crumbs) repeats the receipt-verification product and fee/buyback proposition. Its indexed price ATH is **$0.0004757 on Sep 9, 2026**, equivalent to approximately **$475,700** if using 1B supply. This is a third, conflicting peak, not an alternative independently audited history. Different crawl times, tracked pools or methodologies may explain discrepancies; none was established here.

**Peak-data conclusion:** preserve **$7.97M** as the user-supplied, reported capitalization ATH. Wazz’s screenshot table rounds the selected CRUMBS “case peak” to **$8M**, which is similar in magnitude but belongs to an unverified allegation dashboard. External aggregator price histories do not agree, so the research cannot elevate the reported capitalization into an independently reconciled result. The supplied **$20.06M lifetime trading volume** was also not independently confirmed.

## 7. Accusations, promotion and missing evidence

The supplied third-party screenshots are direct evidence that accusations were published. Wazz alleges **$3.12M extracted from CRUMBS**, a **92-wallet bundle**, and **53 launches / $18.43M extracted** across an alleged wider operation. Other authors allege links to DEED/LEGS/PINK and coordinated influencer calls. The underlying wallet exports, ownership attributions, cost bases and transfer reconciliation were not provided or independently reproduced, so these remain allegations. In particular, the wider $18.43M figure must not be assigned to Crumbs and extraction accusations must not be restated as protocol revenue.

Positive third-party coverage is evidenced by the screenshot corpus: GreenKatana’s reused explainer, Jack’s receipt-rewards explanation, SpiderCrypto’s disclosed position, Fourtis’s automated listing and RH Daily’s ecosystem roundup. **No ad-library entry, invoice, sponsored-placement disclosure or paid influencer agreement was found in this research.** That absence does not prove the campaign had zero paid spend. Eight near-identical voting messages with differing Netlify hosts show an unverified promotional pattern; their affiliation and incentives are unknown.

No reliable product audit, identified development team, verified merchant agreement, executed buyback accounting, actual customer payout total, protocol revenue statement or project profit figure was established. The live outage is an observed availability failure on the research date, not by itself proof of intentional abandonment or fraud.

## Research use

The case is strong evidence for how a simple consumer-finance narrative and cohesive visual campaign can attract speculative attention. It is weaker evidence for sustainable product delivery or economics. Reuse the observable communication methods; use actual payout and treasury records to validate any new product before making economic claims.
