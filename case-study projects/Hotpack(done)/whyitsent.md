# Why HOT Sent

Hotpack used familiar Pokémon imagery to make sealed TCG packs and the choice to hold for fees or rip for cards immediately understandable on Robinhood Chain, combining collectible familiarity with an advertised financial incentive.
Its flame mascot, silver wrappers and white/red block lettering stayed recognizable across nine captured mentions totaling approximately 19,803 X views, alongside Telegram promotion and a Gate Alpha announcement; a lightning badge labeled 100 suggested paid screener boosts, while paid X ads and KOL sponsorship remained unverified.
That attention plausibly supported speculation on the collectible-platform story behind HOT and its reported $2.08M peak capitalization and $6.97M lifetime turnover, although direct token fee rights, delivered pack economics and creator profit were not established by the [research](project-overview.md).
