## Ordering, metrics and transcription rules

Posts are sorted from August 31 to September 9. Same-day order is a reporting order, because no X publication times are visible. The screenshot file order and Top-search order do not establish chronological order. The year **2026** is supported by the profile's join date and the dated Krystal attachment; post date labels themselves omit the year.

Rounded view counters are expanded approximately: 10K → 10,000, 3K → 3,000, 1K → 1,000 and 3.1K → 3,100. Bookmarks are excluded. **0*** means X shows a reply icon without a numeric counter; it is treated as zero for the calculations, rather than claiming to have an exact private analytics count. All nonzero likes/reposts/replies are visibly printed.

Post copy is transcribed as visible, including the provided machine translation and truncation. No missing “Show more” text or unavailable quote is reconstructed. The screenshots contain **no visible videos**. Every post is counted once; metrics within attached dashboards are excluded from X engagement totals.

## Marketing findings from the captured mentions

1. **Trader-led distribution:** the largest captured post is a short call with the CA and a market screenshot, rather than a product tutorial. M05 adds a chart-based Robinhood thesis. Payment or sponsorship for either author is not established.
2. **Repeatable creative:** silver foil packs, familiar Pokémon cards, thick white/red headline text and the flame avatar recur in the profile and two trading screenshots. M06 carries the same flame into a GMGN discovery card. This gives traders recognizable material to redistribute without explaining the whole product.
3. **Possible paid screener promotion:** M01 visibly shows a lightning **100** badge alongside **#17**. It matches the boost convention of the DEX Screener-style interface; [DEX Screener's official Boosting documentation](https://docs.dexscreener.com/boosting) describes boosts as purchased visibility multipliers. This supports an interpretation of paid boost-type exposure, but the platform is not explicitly labeled in the crop and no purchase receipt, payer, spend or attributable reach is available. Team-funded advertising, paid X ads and paid KOL sponsorship are **not verified**.
4. **Yield creative:** M08 uses an unusually large annualized rate and an LP position image as the hook. Its 537 views show exposure to a farming pitch, not proof that the exaggerated rate caused the ATH.
5. **No evidenced incentive campaign:** no screenshot offers rewards for posts, referrals, videos or retweets. Ordinary-looking third-party posts are visible, but their funding status is unknown; they cannot all be certified organic.
6. **Attention included distrust:** the two scam/rug allegations and the later unavailable funding-linked post are material parts of the captured conversation. Neither their allegations nor the claimed funding were independently established.
