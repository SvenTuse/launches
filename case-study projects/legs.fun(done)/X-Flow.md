## Marketing interpretation

The campaign repeats one visual object—glossy lime balloon typography—in a field, sky, aircraft window, blimp, paraglider and website-preview card. White light-weight sans-serif captions supply the punchline; the oversized 3D letters carry recognition. “Leg” means a parlay component, a market advance and something that runs, so the name supports the product explanation and the speculative headline at once.

The launch video supplied most account reach, while the third-leg image elicited the highest interaction/view ratio. The rewards post added an explicit **six-hour, double-reward holder incentive**; it is incentivized product participation, not evidence of paid user-generated posts. Deployment/withdrawal copy provides an operational update but also reveals that withdrawals were being enabled after a rollout; this is not proof of completed or reliable settlement.

There is no evidence in these account screenshots of an X Ads campaign. Disclosed commercial content appears separately in **M14** in [x-mentions-flow.md](x-mentions-flow.md); actual creator payments, paid reach attribution and campaign spend are not supplied.
