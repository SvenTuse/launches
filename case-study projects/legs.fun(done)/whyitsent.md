# Why LEGS Sent

LEGS made Robinhood Chain's memecoin and tokenized-stock speculation immediately understandable as one timed parlay ticket, with the “next leg up” name linking a familiar betting mechanic to the market's existing appetite for PONS-style utility tokens.
Its recognizable lime balloon letters and blue-sky imagery carried a 49-second launch film with roughly 290,000 captured views, while Stitch's 127,000-view analysis supplied most outside reach and Jeff's smaller 5,500-view explainer visibly disclosed a paid partnership; Telegram cross-posts and exchange coverage added distribution, although X ad buying was not established.
That attention appears to have fed speculative demand around the advertised 80% fee-funded buyback-and-burn model and claimed first-hour burn of over 3% of supply, helping explain the reported $6.61M peak capitalization without establishing verified protocol revenue, implemented fee routing or project profit.
