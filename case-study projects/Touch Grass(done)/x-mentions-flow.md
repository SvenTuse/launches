## What the mention evidence supports

AD and DegenCapitalLLC explicitly print the supplied CA, making their Sep 3 posts particularly useful identity matches. DegenCapitalLLC's 5x / 400K-to-2M statement is an author's trading claim; it is not an independently reconstructed return series. Noxxxwill says they personally verified the product, but that remains testimonial evidence. Whales China's translated post ends at “Show more.” Yakuza's Sep 17 post does the same; its earlier Sep 11 quote promotes a Telegram channel. None establishes disclosed fees or sponsorship arrangements.

The 22 solicitation posts vary author, listing ID, countdown threshold and emoji while repeating the same structure. Domains imitate Robinhood with many `robinhood-main-dex-*.netlify.app` variants; CMC-themed examples use `castvote.forum` and `listshelping.forum`. The export provides no evidence that Robinhood or CoinMarketCap owns or endorses these destinations. They were **not visited or interacted with**. The repetition and off-brand destinations support a suspected spam/impersonation classification, not a forensic conclusion about malware or wallet theft. Their votes, listing IDs and purported upcoming listings are unverified.

For a launch team, the actionable lesson is to publish one canonical contract and support path, moderate copied listing solicitations, and track authentic catch videos and venue adoption separately from raw mention count. This sample cannot determine sentiment across the whole community, quantify influencer reach, or prove the token was listed anywhere.
