# Why GRASS Sent

The strongest explanation for GRASS’s rise to a reported $13.43M peak capitalization is that it packaged the “touch grass” meme and the tokenized-stock narrative into “Pokémon GO for stocks,” backed by a demonstrable product where people visited real locations to collect stock-price exposure.

Its marketing combined glossy lime typography, company-logo reward cubes, and consistent sky-and-street imagery across the website and X with demo videos, city reveals, scarce drops, rewarded user clips, and crypto-media coverage; captured account posts accumulated roughly two million displayed views, although paid advertising was not established by the research.

Its buybacks, fee-funded stock rewards, and token-holding requirements gave traders an economic thesis alongside the viral product, helping explain why that attention translated into speculative demand and a substantial valuation.
