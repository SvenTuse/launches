## 1. Official issuer facts materially qualify the narrative

Robinhood's **current chain-specific documentation** describes stock tokens as tokenized debt securities issued by Robinhood Assets (Jersey) Limited. They provide exposure to an underlying security without legal or beneficial rights against its issuer. It documents transferable ERC-20 tokens, 18 decimals and asset-specific Chainlink prices. Thus an on-chain stock token can be programmable loot without making the player an Apple or Tesla shareholder. [Official chain stock-token documentation](https://docs.robinhood.com/chain/stock-tokens/)

The same page restricts offer, sale or delivery to US persons and identifies additional jurisdiction restrictions. This directly conflicts with the project's Sep 4 US-access promotion and its current US FAQ. The report records the contradiction; technical ability to receive an ERC-20 does not settle permitted distribution. [Official distribution restrictions](https://docs.robinhood.com/chain/stock-tokens/)

Robinhood also documents **Classic Stock Tokens**, a different derivative product with different transfer constraints. Touch Grass's terms/support use derivative terminology resembling that product, while its game is presented as transferring chain tokens. Do not merge Classic product rules with the current chain product. Instrument classification and availability should be reconciled against the exact asset used. [Classic product FAQ](https://robinhood.com/eu/en/support/articles/stock-tokens-faq/)

**Implication for a new launch:** the transferable-token architecture is credible as a generic integration pattern, but the promise of literal equity and unrestricted worldwide stock rewards cannot simply be copied. This is a finding about this case's claims, not a recommendation to trade or an independent legal determination.

## 5. What the financial evidence does and does not establish

The user-supplied [project-overview.md](project-overview.md) records **ATH $13.43M** and **lifetime volume $48.52M** without a source, timestamp or exact valuation definition. Preserve them as supplied figures. ATH appears to refer to a valuation/market-cap figure rather than a unit price, but that interpretation is unverified. Neither statistic was independently reconstructed.

Volume is the value traded, not money earned by the creator. ATH valuation is not realized proceeds. Fee-allocation percentages state how fees are divided, not the fee rate itself. The site's proof figures describe buyback expenditure and reward transfers, not operating profit or a creator's realized gain. Sponsorship budgets are largely reward funding, and the page says stock funding is forwarded rather than marked up.

Potential monetization shown by this case consists of a team share of trading fees, a paid shop with a retained share, and possible future venue services. Actual net revenue requires the fee rate/history, authentic volume, shop receipts, treasury spending, gas/infrastructure/support costs and refund liabilities. Those records are not in the supplied material. No defensible profit number can be calculated from $48.52M alone.

## 6. Research conclusions from the additional sources

- A distinctive physical action gives a small token project a story beyond price; third-party commentary repeatedly recognizes that hook.
- Search visibility includes wrong contracts, founder conflation and stale/mixed-time numbers. Match identity and timestamps before importing facts.
- General brand posts and use of a chain are not proof of official endorsement. Neither the supplied screenshots nor the research establishes a Binance listing or Robinhood partnership.
- Paid-promotion budgets, private investment, legal incorporation details, contract audit status, organic user retention and creator profit remain unverified.
- The direct lesson is to make a launch easy to identify, easy to demonstrate and easy to check, then measure human retention and actual business receipts separately from token turnover.
