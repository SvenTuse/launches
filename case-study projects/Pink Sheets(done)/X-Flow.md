## What the structure shows

1. **A recognizable premise before a token address:** the teaser asks why stocks would go onchain without a Pink Sheets desk, then directs readers to a list/site. Its 476K views are 58.77% of all captured official impressions.
2. **Launch converts the premise into an action:** the pinned video adds the contract and the mechanics of counterparties bidding to settle a large order; 172K views are 21.23% of captured impressions. The two videos together supply exactly 80% of the approximate captured view total.
3. **Static creatives make the steps concrete:** the institutional counter, service bell and pink slip turn “desk is open” and “post an intent” into physical actions. P07 has the highest captured post engagement rate at 3.2250%, despite only 24K views; reach and response intensity are different measures.
4. **Token activity becomes social proof:** the $8M creative and 80% fee-funded buyback pledge connect visible token trading with a proposed economic reason to hold. No fee receipts, OTC fills or completed buybacks are shown.
5. **Replies address buying friction and reinforce trader optimism:** Joe receives encouragement, while Josiah receives a precise CA and Fomo/wallet/DEX routing instructions.

The capture supports an effective attention campaign, not attribution of purchases to individual posts, proof of unique organic reach, or a measurement of protocol revenue. There is no visible ad label, sponsorship disclosure or user-reward program in these screenshots; absence of those labels does not prove that promotion was unpaid.
