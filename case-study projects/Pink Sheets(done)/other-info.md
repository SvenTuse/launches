## 2. Fee and value-capture question

An [indexed comment by @zable88](https://www.sotwe.com/zable88?lang=en) says the site displayed “FEE: None - the desk charges nothing” and asks how this fits the official 80% protocol-fee buyback pledge. The same commenter questions how token holders benefit from the proposed desk. This is an attributed report of earlier page wording; the live site's terms could not be checked and no documented answer was retrieved.

The economic question is material: buying PINK on a DEX is not the same activity as executing a block trade through Pink Sheets. High PINK turnover does not establish desk fee income; fee-funded buybacks require an actual fee schedule, collected fees and a demonstrable execution path. The pledge is evidenced in the supplied official screenshot, but implementation is not.
