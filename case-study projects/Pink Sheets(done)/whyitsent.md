# Why PINK Sent

Pink Sheets made the Robinhood stock-token and memecoin narrative immediately legible as a Wall Street-style OTC desk where large orders could bypass thin pools, with pink quotation slips turning the execution pitch into a recognizable identity.
Vintage-office teaser and presenter videos drew approximately 648K captured views, 80% of the official sample, while trader quotes, chart posts and GMGN previews repeated the pink emblem and spread the launch; paid advertising was not established.
That attention plausibly supported speculative demand behind the reported $5.16M peak capitalization, with the promise to allocate 80% of protocol fees to PINK buybacks supplying a token thesis, although the peak was unreconciled and actual fee income or buyback execution was not verified.
