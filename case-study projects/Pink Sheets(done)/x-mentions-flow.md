## Method and counting

Dates are sorted earliest to latest; within Sep 8, screenshot order supplies a reproducible presentation order because posting times are absent. The year 2026 is inferred from the account and research context. Counts are screenshot snapshots; K counters are expanded approximately. An engagement icon without a number is treated as zero displayed interactions, while cropped/hidden counters are marked unavailable rather than invented. Metrics belong to the outer post, never to its quoted post; views are impressions and may overlap across people/posts. Per-post rate = (likes + retweets + comments) / views × 100; the average is the arithmetic mean, while aggregate rate uses summed interactions and views.

The four contextual entries remain in the archive to satisfy complete screenshot coverage, but are excluded from the direct-discussion subtotal. Contract casing is normalized; collapsed text is not supplied from inference. Non-PINK contracts transcribed in M01/M06 are incidental and should not be used as Pink Sheets identifiers.
